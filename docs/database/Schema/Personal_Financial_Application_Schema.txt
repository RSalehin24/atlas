Categories: [ self-referencing, 1 to many, for subcategories ]
* Name
* Parent Category [optional]
* Tax Head [FK ― Tax Spending Head, optional]
* Type [determines how the app treats this for tax & net worth]
   * Income       — earned money (salary, interest, rent, business, capital gains)
   * Expense      — consumption spending (maps to IT-10BB expenditure heads)
   * Transfer     — moving between own accounts or cash ↔ bank (no tax impact)
   * Investment   — acquiring financial assets (DPS, FDR, shares, Sanchayapatra, CFD, insurance)
