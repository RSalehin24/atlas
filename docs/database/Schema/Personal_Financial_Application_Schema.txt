PERSONAL FINANCIAL ATLAS — COMPLETE DATA SCHEMA (v2)
=====================================================
Supersedes all previous drafts. Read the authoritative .md version for full details.

CONVENTIONS
- Money: INTEGER in poisha (taka x 100). Never REAL.
- IDs: INTEGER autoincrement (single-device). All tables have created_at, updated_at, is_deleted.
- Enums: TEXT with CHECK constraints.
- Computed fields marked [computed] — never hand-edited.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Bank
----------------------------------------------------------------------
id [PK], name, institution_type [BANK|MFS|NBFI], swift_code, logo_url, is_deleted

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2 Branch
----------------------------------------------------------------------
id [PK], bank_id [FK->Bank], name, routing_number, address, district, contact_number, is_deleted

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3 Account
----------------------------------------------------------------------
id [PK], bank_id [FK->Bank], branch_id [FK->Branch, nullable], identifier_name,
account_number, type [SAVINGS|CURRENT|DPS|FDR|LOAN], account_holder_name,
mobile_number, email, currency [def:BDT], current_balance, overdraft_limit [CURRENT],
status [ACTIVE|CLOSED|MATURED|DEFAULTED], opening_date, is_deleted

current_balance: savings/current = ledger; DPS/FDR = accumulated; LOAN = outstanding principal

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2.4 AccountTermDetail (1:1 with Account, type=FDR|DPS)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
account_id [PK/FK->Account], start_date, maturity_date, tenure_months, interest_rate,

maturity_amount [computed], auto_renewal, principal_amount [FDR],
installment_amount [DPS], installment_date [DPS, 1-31],
total_principal_on_maturity [DPS, computed], is_tax_rebate_eligible [DPS, def:false]

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2.5 LoanDetail (1:1 with Account, type=LOAN)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
account_id [PK/FK->Account], loan_type [PERSONAL|AUTO|HOME|CREDIT_CARD_EMI|INFORMAL|OTHER],
disbursed_date, start_date, end_date, principal_amount, interest_rate, processing_fee,
emi_amount, installment_date, duration_months, total_emi_payable [computed],
total_paid [computed from transactions], repayment_status [ONGOING|REPAID|DEFAULTED],
is_interest_tax_deductible [def:false], tax_deductible_interest_this_year

2.25 TaxYear
----------------------------------------------------------------------
id [PK], financial_year [e.g. "2026-2027"], tax_profile_id [FK->TaxProfile],
status [DRAFT|COMPUTED|FILED], notes,
--- Income ---

standard_deduction_applied [computed],

--- Tax ---
tax_before_rebate [computed], minimum_tax_applied,
--- Interest ---
vat_on_interest [computed],
--- TPS ---
advance_tax_paid [computed = SUM TpsDeduction],
--- Rebate ---
total_rebate_claimed [computed], max_possible_rebate [computed],
investment_required_for_max_rebate [computed],
--- Clawback ---
rebate_clawback_amount [computed],
--- Surcharge ---
surcharge_amount [computed],
--- Filing ---
filing_incentive_amount [computed], filing_penalty_amount [computed],
--- Final ---
net_tax_payable [computed],
is_deleted
